## Style 11 animations
one-big-slam, splits, heavy-landing, rollout, dot-hop, sticker-stack, slingshot, traced-outline, plucked-string, tumbling-letter, plus spotlight. Use 4–7 typography moments per minute (not every sentence).

## Style 12 modules
glass-hook, glass-broll, glass-bento, glass-track, glass-type, glass-toggles, glass-spotlight-word, glass-ots, glass-stack, glass-checklist, glass-verdict, glass-outro, glass-captions.

## Choosing quickly
- Serious/data → 2, 3, 4, 12
- Fun/young → 5, 8, 10, 7
- Calm/aesthetic → 6, 9
- Podcast/interview → 11 (or 12 for a premium feel)
- The user can always force a style ("pakai style 7"). You may also mix: keep one base style and borrow at most 1–2 effects from another, only if the palette stays consistent.
